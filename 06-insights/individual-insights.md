# Individual Insights: Strategy Development Process

> Module 6 · Leverage AI Tools for Leadership Execution
>
> Your personal reflection on building the strategy end to end.

## Friction points

_What was the hardest part of building this strategy, and how did you work through it?_

> Separating product intent from current architecture.

> Turning engagement into credible retention economics.

> Making “trusted” operational rather than aspirational.

## Key learnings

_A few surprising discoveries or insights you gained from the course overall._

> Lead with choices and trade-offs.

> Treat architecture evolution as part of product planning.

> Keep accountability human.

> Scale only when evidence earns it.

## "Aha!" moment

_Your main "aha" moment during the project process._

> Strategy, roadmap, charter and model are not separate artifacts. They are one decision system: every delivery choice should trace to an outcome and every investment should have a stop rule.
