# Product Strategy One-Pager & OKRs, Module 1

- **Scenario:** My own initiative

## Playing to Win cascade

| Question | Choice |
|---|---|
| Winning aspiration | Transform MyCF from a system customers use into the operational system they depend on by making conversation the primary interface to fleet intelligence.
Our strategic hypothesis is that fleet managers will increasingly choose conversational interaction as their primary operational workflow because it reduces the time and cognitive effort required to make trusted fleet decisions.
If this hypothesis proves true, the AI Assistant will increase customer retention, platform engagement and customer lifetime value by embedding MyCF into customers' daily operational decisions rather than occasional reporting workflows.
Unlike a feature designed to increase adoption, the AI Assistant is a strategic capability whose purpose is to make MyCF progressively more indispensable after onboarding. |
| Where to play | Existing MyCF customers who manage fleets within the platform and have sufficient operational data to support trusted, evidence-backed operational answers.
We compete inside authenticated workflows where proprietary fleet data creates differentiated value.
We deliberately sequence breadth before depth. Phase 1 delivers universal capabilities valuable across every fleet segment, followed by HGV-specific operational workflows in H2 2027. Geography-specific capabilities remain intentionally deferred until after 2027 so that platform-wide value is maximised before investing in localisation. |
| How to win | How to win
We become the fastest and most trusted operational interface for fleet managers.
Customers should reach for the AI Assistant before navigating reports, dashboards or documentation because it consistently provides faster, evidence-backed answers grounded in their own fleet data while reducing the cognitive effort required to make operational decisions.
Over time, the assistant evolves from answering questions to proactively identifying operational opportunities, risks and recommendations.
The competitive advantage is not conversational AI itself.
The competitive advantage is making MyCF the easiest place to convert proprietary operational data into trusted business decisions.
Strategic hypothesis
This strategy is based on one central hypothesis: fleet managers will increasingly adopt conversational interaction as their preferred operational workflow when it consistently reduces the time and cognitive effort required to make trusted decisions. As this behaviour becomes habitual, MyCF will become more deeply embedded in customers' daily operations, increasing engagement, customer retention and customer lifetime value.
Our management system will continuously test this hypothesis through behavioural adoption, customer value and commercial outcomes. |

## One hard no
We will not generate authoritative operational answers unless they are grounded in trusted MyCF or approved third-party data.
This preserves customer trust, protects our differentiation and ensures every investment strengthens MyCF's position as the authoritative operational system rather than becoming a generic AI assistant.

## OKR cascade
- **Objective:** Increase customer lifetime value by making MyCF the indispensable operational interface customers rely on daily for fleet decisions.
- **KR1:** Increase annual customer retention from 88% to 92%.
- **KR2:** Increase Net Revenue Retention from 102% to 110%.
- **KR3:** Increase average Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) by 20% by 31 December 2027.
