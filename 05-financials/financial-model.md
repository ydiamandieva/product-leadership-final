## 2. Kill criteria

Strategic investment should stop when evidence demonstrates that conversational interaction does not improve customer economics.
Terminate further strategic investment in expanding AI Assistant capabilities if, within 12 months of General Availability:

- fewer than 30% of eligible customer accounts demonstrate sustained weekly operational use of the AI Assistant;
cohort analysis shows no statistically significant improvement in Annual Customer Retention or Net Revenue Retention compared with comparable non-adopting customers;
- projected Customer Lifetime Value uplift is insufficient to maintain an LTV:CAC ratio above 3:1; or
- cumulative incremental CLV generated by AI-enabled customers is not expected to exceed cumulative AI operating costs within the agreed investment horizon.

If any of these conditions are met, the strategic hypothesis that conversational interaction creates durable commercial value is considered disproven. Investment should be limited to maintenance and contractual obligations, with no further strategic expansion until a revised business case demonstrates an acceptable financial return.
