## 1. Stakeholder map

_The key players affected by your initiative, their decision power and influence, potential blockers, and how you'll bring each along._

| Stakeholder | Power / influence | Stance (champion / neutral / blocker) | How you'll bring them along |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
|Head of Product / Product Leadership|Very High – Owns product strategy, investment and roadmap prioritisation.|Champion (if commercial value is demonstrated)|Tie every investment to retention, NRR and CLV. Use quarterly strategy reviews and business-case evidence to show progress against OKRs.|
| PM 1 – Onboarding | Medium – Owns activation journey and shared engineering capacity. | Neutral | Position AI as improving activation, not competing with onboarding. Define clear ownership: they own the journey, AI team owns the capability. |
| PM 2 – Retention | Medium – Owns engagement and retention initiatives. | Champion | Demonstrate that habitual AI usage supports retention goals. Share success metrics and jointly prioritise AI-enabled retention experiences. |
| PM 3 – AI Check-in Layer | Medium – Owns an experience built on top of the AI capability. | Neutral | Establish clear interfaces and decision rights. Co-design shared experiences while keeping AI behaviour owned centrally. |
| Engineering Manager / Engineering Team | High – Controls engineering capacity and delivery feasibility. | Neutral | Prioritise outcomes over features, agree realistic sequencing, and involve engineering early in trade-off decisions. |
| Head of Data Science / Data Science Team | High – Owns AI quality, models and technical direction. | Neutral (potential blocker if priorities diverge) | Align on product intent before implementation. Jointly define quality standards, evaluation metrics and architectural evolution. Make technical constraints explicit rather than allowing them to redefine product strategy. |
| Growth Manager | Medium – Influences experimentation and behavioural adoption. | Champion | Collaborate on experiments that validate behavioural change. Share telemetry and jointly define adoption metrics. |
| Customer Success Lead | Medium – Influences customer adoption, renewals and qualitative feedback. | Champion | Involve them in pilots, collect structured customer feedback, and demonstrate measurable customer value that supports renewals. |
